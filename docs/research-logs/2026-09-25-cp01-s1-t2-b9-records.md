## Challenge plan and why

The risks are two record validators disagreeing on an unlisted structural rule, and an identity grammar implemented by splitting on every colon.

- **Case/clause mapping:** each AC14 case maps to a Core §6 sentence (L247–L249), and each AC13 case to Core §9 L357.
- **Contradiction check:** S02 has no crosswalk quote about storing records; the S02 quotes were read. AC01's change therefore removes no original obligation.
- **YAML parse and `contracts:check`:** both run. One plain scalar containing `: ` was found and rephrased.
