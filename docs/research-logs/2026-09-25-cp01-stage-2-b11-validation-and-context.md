| Q28 — which provenance record rule 17 and the historical check read: **missing requirement, pinned at contract level** | No Checkpoint 1 step writes execution provenance (the released runtime wrote none: `git grep provenance 207ac08 -- src` matches a comment only). Replay validation is requested by supplying a replay-base document, a YAML mapping of `repository_revision`, `project_graph_revision` and `environment_lock_hash` read through the canonical loader; it is Checkpoint 1's `validate` input, not a universal provenance schema, and later operation owners (Core §56: "the operation owner declares its provenance inputs", Graph Review in Spec 04) record the tuple in their own provenance. The historical policy check reads `repository_revision` from the same document (S09/R04, R06, AC04, AC05). For read-only `validate`, "refused without an attempt or replay record" means it reports the refusal, leaves the working tree, index and refs untouched and creates no restore location or artefact. S19/AC11's "execution provenance recording a sha256 graph revision" is the same document form with a legacy identity. The document is supplied by path and decoded by a registered decoder of the canonical loader; validate reports the recorded `environment_lock_hash` without verifying it, since Step 15 owns lock identity (S09/AC05 `differing-lock-hash`). Rejected: deferring rule 17 (the exit gate requires all 17 rules); a provenance store (Core §59 forbids a snapshot database). |
| Q29 — default context contents, explicit history, contribution rules: **already specified, made explicit** | Default Delivery context is the direction's current Intent, Decision, Contract and Brief as complete records in lineage order, excluding rejected alternatives, superseded records, other directions' records, execution transcripts and execution-state detail (Core §§3, 8, 13, 39; released `context.ts` returned the same set). A caller requires excluded history by naming record IDs; they return in a separate history section, each marked with its superseding record, never merged into the current lineage. Extension contributions are keyed by Extension ID under the Core §54 grammar, `core` reserved, ordered by Extension ID; no size rule is invented (S09/R09, R10, AC08, AC09). Rejected: omitting the Decision (its outcome and actor are the recorded authority the Contract rests on); free-text history requests (unbounded and unverifiable). |
| Q30 — exit status, output categories, historical incompatibility, unavailable authority: **partly specified; the rest pinned at contract level** | `validate` exits non-zero when any minimum rule fails, the load is incomplete or a requested replay base cannot be verified, and zero otherwise. Each problem carries a category: structure failure, unsupported or inactive component, unavailable verification or unavailable execution authority (Core §57's three diagnostic distinctions plus the replay/historical "unavailable verification" it names). Historical incompatibility of a withdrawn Decision is a compatibility result and never a failure (Core §57: "not failure of the old actor under today's rules"). Unavailable execution authority means the policy needed to authorise a current Decision or Gate cannot be determined: lifecycle configuration missing or unloadable, reported with its load problem, or a historical check without a reconstructible revision (S09/R11, AC11). A machine-readable output form is left to implementation; the released `--json` flag is the obvious choice but no clause requires it. Rejected: warnings that change the exit status (no clause defines validate warnings; doctor owns healthy/warning/action-required, Distribution §16). |

Checks: (1) confirm no Checkpoint 1 contract or step prose writes provenance (`grep -n provenance CP01-S*.yml 01-self-hosted-delivery.md`: S05, S09, S15 and S19 mention it and none writes it); (2) confirm Core §56's tuple names exactly the three identities pinned and nothing else; (3) confirm S12/AC08 and S10/R05 consume the context definition without naming record sets that differ from S09/R09; (4) confirm Step 23 and Step 25 prose run `pactwright validate` and expect it to "pass", which requires an exit-status contract; (5) confirm S09/AC06's `incomplete-load` case and R07 already forbid a whole-graph pass, so AC11 adds the exit-status observation rather than new meaning. All hold.
