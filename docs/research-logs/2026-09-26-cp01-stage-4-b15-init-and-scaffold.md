| Question / classification | Adopted answer; alternatives rejected |
|---|---|
| Q42 — lock of a plain scaffold; incomplete load; hashability: **missing requirement, resolved in Distribution v5 §§3, 12** | Plain `init` writes the §12 `version: 1` lock with `runtime.version` and an empty `extensions` mapping and no `agent_pack`, `agents` or `skills`; those three keys are present exactly when a pack is selected. Core §§54 and 56 are therefore unchanged: every required input exists, the scaffold is a complete load, its empty graph has the pg1 empty-graph revision (S14/AC02 `empty-graph-revision`) and its lock yields an `environment_lock_hash` (B17; S15/AC11 `unactivated-scaffold`). Executability is a separate condition: `sync`, `lifecycle run` and every capability invocation refuse the scaffold naming the missing selection (S14/AC02, S17/AC03 `unactivated-scaffold`), and `doctor` reports no selected pack as action required (B18; S18/AC02 `no-pack-selected`). Rejected: making the lock optional for scaffolds (would amend Core §§54/56 and relax S01/AC09 and S05/AC12, which the Stage 1 exit review forbade); hashing the scaffold's environment as absent (a resolved runtime with no pack is an exact environment and replay of an unactivated scaffold is not needed, so nothing is gained by a special case). |
| Q41 — owner and form of the selection input; what Step 14 exercises before Step 16; refused one-shot init: **ambiguous requirement, resolved in Distribution v5 §3** | Distribution §3 owns init's options, as it already named `--with` and `--github`; the runbook was right not to invent one. The input is `pactwright init --agent-pack <source>`, with `<source>` in the §5 `agent-pack use` grammar, and it composes plain init with `agent-pack use <source>`, so `init --agent-pack` is itself the one-shot composition Step 14 exercises before Step 16 adds `extension add` through `--with` (S14/R04, AC03; S16/R10, AC14 run `init --with <ext> --agent-pack <source>`). A refused init, whether for a `--with` without selection, an incompatible or unresolvable selection or an Extension that cannot be installed, validates the whole requested composition first and leaves no scaffold, no pack, and the package manifest and package-manager lock byte-identical after removing any dependency the attempt added (S14/R04, R08, AC04 `incompatible-selection`, `unresolvable-source`; S16/AC15). Plain `init` with no option is not a refusal: it creates the scaffold (S14/AC04 `missing-selection`). `init` never prompts and never defaults a pack (S14/R03, AC08). Init on an initialised project preserves everything; a selection named there is applied through `agent-pack use` only when no pack is selected and refused when it differs (S14/R05, AC05 `differing-selection`, `select-on-scaffold`). Rejected: an interactive prompt (not composable by the harness, Checkpoint 2's `init --github` fixtures or Step 23's scripted consumer, and §5 forbids recommending into a silent selection); leaving the scaffold behind on refusal (the explicit path's end state after a refused `use` is a scaffold, but one-shot init's request was an activated environment, and the Guide's filesystem-mutation rule and S14/R08 forbid a partial result of a refused mutation). |

**Challenge plan and why:** the wrong implementations are an init that quietly selects `@pactwright/standard` because it is installed, a scaffold that the loader or hasher treats as incomplete, and a refused one-shot init that leaves a half-set-up repository or an installed package behind. Checks: (1) `git show 207ac08:src/init.ts` confirms the released init selected standard through its template and wrote a resolved lock, so the redesign's "no implicit selection" is a real behaviour change that S14/AC01, AC04 and AC05 must distinguish, and that a lock at init is released behaviour; (2) `grep -n "agent-pack use\|--with\|--github\|selection input" docs/checkpoints/0*.md docs/specs/02-*.md` confirms Checkpoint 2 lines 449–451 and Step 23 consume "the documented normal init selection interaction/input established in Checkpoint 1" without naming a form, so a Distribution-owned option satisfies them; (3) Step 29's prose ran `init` then `sync` with no selection, which under S14/R03 can never pass, so the pass adds `agent-pack use @pactwright/standard` there (Checkpoint 1 v29) as a consequence of the already-drafted Stage 4 requirement; Steps 23 and 25 already select explicitly; (4) walk Core §54's required-path table and §56's "any failed required input" rule to confirm a scaffold with the runtime-only lock satisfies both without amendment; (5) confirm S01/AC09 `missing-lock` and S05/AC12 keep their meaning, since the scaffold has a lock. All five hold on the amended text.
