# CP01 Stages 6–11 B26 — Kakeibo consumer root, foundation and financial-domain acceptance

**Scope:** batch B26 of the Checkpoint 1 Stages 6–11 pass: Q63 and Q64. Revisions, authorisation and method are as in [B22](./2026-09-27-cp01-stages-6-11-b22-packed-consumer-proofs.md). **Dependencies:** B25 (published `0.0.2`), B22 (adapter session and approval pattern). **Owning clauses:** Kakeibo Acceptance Profile §§1–5, 16, 17; Guide §Kakeibo acceptance model, §Execution location, §Test layers; Distribution §§3, 5, 14, 15, 16; Core §§8, 12, 19, 44, 46, 57; Principles §§3, 8, 12, 16, 17; Checkpoint 1 Stage 10, §4; Spec 00 §§3, 4. **Affected:** Stage 10 intro, Steps 29 and 30.

| Question / classification | Adopted answer; alternatives rejected |
|---|---|
| Q63 — who establishes the foundation; Kakeibo write authority; spec revisions: **contradictory requirement, resolved in Stage 10 and Step 29 and 30 prose** | The Stage 10 intro permits only the pnpm package/workspace root as pre-Pactwright bootstrap, while profile §5 also asks to establish or verify the public-repository foundation. Both hold if the foundation is not pre-Pactwright work: Step 29 creates only the bootstrap files where absent and records whether each foundation item exists; Step 30 delivers each absent item through its own Kakeibo Delivery lineage, which is Principles §3 applied to Kakeibo once Pactwright is installed. The canonical `docs/specs/` set is the exception: specifications are authority, not Delivery output (Guide §Kakeibo acceptance model), so if it is absent Stage 10 pauses as missing authority (Spec 00 §4). Preserving an existing `package.json` means keeping it apart from the fields the package manager writes on `pnpm add`, since byte-for-byte preservation is impossible. The Kakeibo repository is an external target: its writes and default-branch commits need the Kakeibo owner's authority, and the run configuration names the repository and its starting revision (Spec 00 §3). Step 29 records the revisions of `docs/specs/README.md` and the 02, 05 and 06 specifications that govern Stage 10. Rejected: hand-writing LICENSE and SECURITY.md at Step 29 (contradicts the Stage 10 intro); deferring the foundation to Checkpoint 2 (the profile ties it to Checkpoint 1); requiring a `packageManager` field (a lockfile makes detection unambiguous under Distribution §15). |
| Q64 — deciding actor; list versus specification; verifiable checks; invariant preservation: **ambiguous requirement, resolved in Step 30 prose** | The Kakeibo owner approves the Contract. The thirteen invariants are the profile's §4 "Financial truth" list plus "preparation state does not create reviewed truth" from §4 "Review truth"; the specification at the recorded revision governs, so where they differ the Contract states the specification's invariant, its test replaces the listed one, and the difference becomes a Step 31 finding. Verification is executable: each invariant's tests pass on the delivered code and fail on a variant, supplied by the verifier and not the producer, that violates only it (Spec 00 §3 forbids a producer weakening its own checks); two runs give identical results; domain logic reads no clock, randomness, network or filesystem and imports none of the excluded packages. Preservation through the lineage is Principles §17's "a Contract loses a financial invariant", checked by review tracing each invariant through Contract, Brief, Review and Evidence. Rejected: an aggregate green test run as the invariant proof; Pactwright evaluation cases for Kakeibo invariants (domain quality stays with the project unless Step 31 finds a repeatable Pactwright responsibility failure). |

**Challenge plan and why:** the wrong implementations are a Step 29 that scaffolds infrastructure or edits Kakeibo specifications, a model-authored specification set treated as authority, a Step 30 whose tests pass while an invariant has dropped out of the Contract, a domain package reading the clock or importing a Cloudflare binding, and a Kakeibo write made without the owner's authority. Checks: (1) the profile §5 text and the Stage 10 intro, side by side, are both satisfied by the split; (2) Checkpoint 2 v18 Step 17 starts Kakeibo at exact `0.0.2` with `packages/domain` present, matching Stage 10's end state; (3) the invariant list matches profile §4 in substance; (4) each semantic named in the Step 30 Intent has an invariant or an explicit Contract obligation; (5) Distribution §15 makes a lockfile sufficient for detection. All hold. The Kakeibo repository was not read in this session, which is why Step 29 inventories its state rather than assuming it.
